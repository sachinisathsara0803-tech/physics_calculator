Gravity Calculator

Explore the force that keeps our universe together

Gravity is an invisible force that draws all objects with mass or energy toward each other. This calculator allows you to calculate gravity according to Isaac Newton's law of universal gravitation. According to Newton's view, gravity is a universal force of attraction between any two objects based on their masses and the distance between them.

If you want to explore the formula used to calculate gravity, click the "More Info" button below.

Newton's Universal Gravitation
\[ F = G \frac{m_1m_2}{r^2} \]

F = Gravitational force between two objects, measured in Newton (N).

G = Universal gravitational constant, approximately \(6.674 \times 10^{-11}\, N\cdot m^2/kg^2\).

\(m_1\) = Mass of the first object, measured in kilograms (kg).

\(m_2\) = Mass of the second object, measured in kilograms (kg).

r = Distance between the centers of the two masses, measured in meters (m).
