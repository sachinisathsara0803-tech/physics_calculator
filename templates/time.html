Gravitational Time Dilation
\[ t' = \frac{t}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{2GM}{rc^2}}} \]

\(t_{0}\) = Proper time measured by an observer deep in the gravitational field (closer to the mass).

\(t_{f}\) =Coordinate time measured by an observer at an infinite distance away from the mass (where gravity is zero).

G = Universal gravitational constant \(6.674 \times 10^{-11}\, N\cdot m^2/kg^2\).

M = Mass of the object creating the gravitational field (kg).

r = Radial distance from the center of the mass to the observer.

c = Speed of light in a vacuum (\(\approx 3 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}\)).
