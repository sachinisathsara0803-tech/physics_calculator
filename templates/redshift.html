Gravitational Redshift (by using physical data)
\[ z = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{2GM}{rc^2}}} - 1 \]

Z = z is the redshift parameter.

G = Universal gravitational constant, approximately \(6.674 \times 10^{-11}\, N\cdot m^2/kg^2\).

M = Mass of the compact object(kg).

r = Radial distance from the center of mass to the emission point.

c = Speed of light in a vacuum.
