Escape Velocity
\[ v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{R}} \]

Ve = Escape velocity (or speed).

G = Universal gravitational constant \(6.674 \times 10^{-11}\, N\cdot m^2/kg^2\).

M = Mass of the central body being orbited (like Earth or a star) in kilograms (kg).

R = Distance from the center of the central body to the orbiting object in meters (m).
