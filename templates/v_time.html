Velocity Time Dilation
\[ t' = \frac{t}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}} \]

t' = The time interval measured by an observer moving relative to the event (dilated or longer time).

t0 = The proper time interval measured by an observer at rest relative to the event (shorter time).

v =The relative velocity of the moving object or observer.

c = The speed of light in a vacuum (\(3.0 \times 10^8\) m/s).
